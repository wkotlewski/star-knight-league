# Star Knight League

A space-sports learning game for a 3rd grader. Build your ship, pick your crew, and win the Station Cup against the Eclipse Commander's Armor Troopers by solving math problems along the way.

Plays in any browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. No install. Progress saves on each device.

## Play

Play it at **https://wkotlewski.github.io/star-knight-league/**

To host your own copy, turn on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → `main` / root). It will be at:

`https://wkotlewski.github.io/<repo-name>/`

On a phone or tablet, use **Add to Home Screen** so it opens like an app. After the first load it also works offline.

## Players

Each player signs in with a username and a 4-number PIN. Every username has its own points, trophy points, prizes, and progress, so someone else playing on the same device can't change hers.

- Usernames ignore capital letters and extra spaces ("Amaya" and "amaya" are the same player).
- Usernames can't be taken: a new name that matches an existing player once spaces and numbers are ignored ("Amaya2", "A maya") is turned down. Typing an existing name opens that player's game; it never makes a second copy.
- **PIN**: new players make a PIN right after picking a username, typing it twice to confirm. Players made before PINs existed set one the next time they sign in. The PIN is stored, scrambled, with that username on the device.
- **Forgot the PIN?** A grown-up taps **Forgot PIN?** and types one of the parent phone numbers saved for that player, then she makes a new PIN. If no phone number is saved, a grown-up presses and holds **Reset PIN** for 3 seconds.
- **Change PIN**: in the Grown-ups corner.
- Tap the name in the top bar to switch players. A device remembers the signed-in player until the browser tab is closed.
- **Updating from an older version:** the game saved before usernames existed goes to the first username made on that device. The old save also stays as a backup.
- Saves live in that browser on that device. Clearing the browser's site data erases them, and they don't move between devices.
- **Back up and restore**: in the Grown-ups corner, **Back up and send** makes a small game file (points, trophy points, prizes, PIN, and progress) and opens the share sheet, so you can pick Mail and email it to yourself. On browsers that can't share files, it saves the file and opens a pre-filled email to attach it to. **Restore a backup** (on the sign-in screen and in the Grown-ups corner) loads that file back, on a cleared device or a new one. Restoring over an existing player shows both scores and asks before replacing.
- On iPhone and iPad, add the game to the Home Screen and open it from there. Safari can clear saved data for websites that haven't been opened in about 7 days, and Home Screen apps are exempt.

## Mixed subjects

Every chapter mixes **math, spelling, word meanings, and reading**, so she practices all four whichever game she likes best. About a third of the questions come from that chapter's own subject. The rest lean toward the subjects she gets wrong most or has practiced least. Within each subject, the question types she misses come up more often. Question titles stay with the game (for example, "Power Pitch! Spell it right to charge your blade bat."). Grown-ups can switch back to **each game's own subject**.

## Chapters

Pick a chapter from the title screen. **Play** continues the last chapter she played.

### Chapter 1: The Station Cup (Asteroid Baseball · math)

- **Create your Star Knight**: six diverse looks, your own name, and a blade color.
- **Hangar**: the ship frame and cargo bay give powers in every game. Paint, droid, and crew are for style. Paint colors and droid heads unlock with credits.

  | Choice | Power |
  | --- | --- |
  | Swift Scout | Warning arrows stay longer, pitches come a little slower, and the basketball shot clock gets 2 more seconds |
  | Heavy Hauler | A shield blocks one mistake each match (strike three, an opponent's hit, a tackle, their goal or basket, or her missed shot) |
  | Star Cruiser | +5 credits after every match |
  | Science Lab | A Hint button on questions removes a wrong choice, fills the first digit, or places the next letter |
  | Rescue Pod | One free retry on a missed question each match |
  | Repair Drones | Bigger green zones for swinging, pitching, and shooting, and a weaker goalie |
  | Sports Gear | Power-ups earned from questions last one extra play |

  The match screen for each Cup shows her current ship powers, with a shortcut to the Hangar.
- **The Station Cup**: a ladder of five teams (Armor Troopers, Moon Miners, Comet Crew, Nebula Nine, and the Eclipse Elite). Beat a team to unlock the next one.
- **Each match is one round each way**:
  - **Bat**: tap **Swing** when the ball reaches the glowing ring. After a hit, she runs the bases herself. At each base she decides **Run!** or **Stop** before the timer runs out, while the fielder has the ball. Running for an extra base earns more credits, and reaching home is an inside-the-park home run, but the throw can beat her. When she's on base, she may get a chance to steal.
  - **Then she chooses Pitch or Catch** for the other team's turn.
    - **Pitch**: pick a Fastball, Curveball, or Changeup, then tap **Throw** when the marker is in the green.
    - **Catch**: move the glove Left, Middle, or Right to where the ball is going. Throw out runners who try to steal.
    - **Fielding (both roles)**: when they get a hit, her teammate fields it and she taps **1st**, **2nd**, **3rd**, or **Home** to throw to the base a runner is heading to before the timer runs out. Bases with runners heading to them are circled.
  - A tie plays one more round.
- **Math pops up at least once each turn** (batting, pitching, or catching), plus a few extra times depending on the grown-ups setting:
  - **Power Pitch** charges the blade bat.
  - **Steal the base** gets her runner in safe.
  - **Star Pitch** or **Call the pitch** throws a pitch that's very hard to hit.
  - **Steal alert** throws out a runner.

### Chapter 2: Meteor Storm (Hover-Soccer · spelling)

- **The Hover Cup**: the same five teams, on an ice-moon soccer field. A meteor storm knocked out the station's shields, and every win earns a shield crystal.
- **Each match is three turns each way**:
  - **Attack**: tap Left, Middle, or Right to dodge defenders. A red arrow warns when a defender is about to move. Two teammates run alongside her, and **Pass** sends the ball to the most open one (a nearby defender can intercept). At the top, tap **Shoot** when the aim arrow points at a corner.
  - **Then she chooses Goalie or Defender** for the other team's three turns.
    - **Goalie**: watch which way the kicker leans, then dive Left, Middle, or Right.
    - **Defender**: stay in front of the striker to tackle the ball. Their attackers pass to each other too, so watch the warning arrows.
  - A tie goes to a penalty shootout.
- **A spelling word pops up at least once each half**, plus extras depending on the grown-ups setting:
  - **Defender closing in!** gives hover boots that jump the next tackle.
  - **Meteor Kick** makes the goalie much slower.
  - **Super Save** shows where their shot is going.
  - **Slide tackle** makes her tackle reach wider.
- **Spelling words** come from the weekly list in the Grown-ups corner when there is one, otherwise from a built-in bank of about 90 grade-3 words. The bank covers vowel teams, r-controlled vowels, silent letters, -ed/-ing, plurals, compound words, prefixes and suffixes, and homophones. Each word comes with a sentence. **Hear it** reads the word aloud, and **Peek** flashes it for two seconds. She taps letter tiles (with a few decoys) or picks the right spelling from four choices. Missed words come back later.

### Chapter 3: The Lost Signal (Zero-G Basketball · vocabulary)

- **The Hoops League**: the same five teams, on a top-down half court like soccer. Every win earns a decoder chip to fix the station's radio dish.
- **Offense (3 possessions)**: she controls whoever has the ball. She taps anywhere on the court to move and taps a teammate to pass. Teammates cut to new spots, and the open ones glow green. Defenders guard each player, and a defender standing in a passing lane can intercept. Then she taps **Shoot** and taps again when the marker is in the green. Shots from beyond the 3-point line are worth 3, and shots near the hoop are easiest. An open shot gets a bigger green zone than a guarded one, and the shot clock keeps it moving.
- **Defense (3 possessions)**: she controls her own defender and taps anywhere to move. Two teammates guard the other players. The other team passes away from pressure, and if she stands in the passing lane she intercepts. When she's close to the ball handler, she can **Steal** (tap when the dribble hits the ring) or **Block** (tap when they shoot; watch for pump fakes). If she's out of position, they get an open shot.
- A tie goes to overtime.
- **Word questions** at least once each half, plus surprises: **Laser Shot** (bigger green zone), **Double-team!** (a safe pass to the most open teammate), **Super Defense** (more time to steal or block), and **Loose ball!**
- **About 90 questions** aligned to California grade 3 language standards (L.3.1, L.3.4, L.3.5): word meanings, context clues, synonyms, antonyms, prefixes and suffixes, homophones, parts of speech, irregular past-tense verbs, and comparing with -er/-est.

### Chapter 4: Eclipse Ambush (space battle · mixed review)

- **Five missions** against each team's fleet. The last one is the Eclipse Commander's flagship, with a big shield bar.
- She flies **her own Hangar ship** and taps **Left**, **Middle**, or **Right** to get out of the red warning lanes. Her ship fires on its own.
- **Three waves per mission.** Between waves she picks **Shields** (+1 heart) or **Blasters** (fire faster).
- **Mixed review**: at least one question each wave, randomly math, spelling, or vocabulary. A right answer charges the **Mega Laser**, which hits every ship when she taps **Mega**. When she takes a hit, a surprise question can repair a shield.

### Chapter 5: The Missing Droid (rescue run · reading)

- Little Cadet's droid is missing. **Five places** on its trail: Glow Caves, Lava Moon, Ice Rings, Comet Trail, and the Eclipse Base, where she finds the droid helping fix an engine.
- **Jetpack run**: tap **Up**, **Middle**, or **Down** to dodge rocks, crystals, and meteors and grab gears. There are three hearts and three legs per mission.
- **Between legs** she picks the **Cave tunnel** (more gears, more rocks) or the **Sky bridge** (calmer).
- **Reading**: every leg starts with a short passage, a clue on the droid's trail. A right answer turns on the tractor beam, which pulls in gears. A surprise "Rock slide!" passage can give a shield bubble.
- **24 original passages, 48 questions** aligned to California grade 3 reading standards (RL/RI 3.1–3.4): key details, main idea and lesson, order of events, characters and feelings, words in context, cause and effect, and inferences. **Read it to me** reads the passage aloud. Weaker skills come up more often, and missed questions come back.

### Prize Shop (real-life rewards)

- **Trophy points** come only from wins, and tougher teams give more: Troopers 4, Miners 6, Comets 9, Nebula 12, Elite 18. The first win against a team gives double, and the first Cup win adds 20. A daily limit (default 30, adjustable) keeps the big prizes months away.
- **Prizes**, cheapest first. Parents can edit, add, or remove prizes.

  | Prize | Points |
  | --- | --- |
  | Chocolate candy | 40 |
  | Get out of folding clothes pass | 60 |
  | 30 minutes of video games | 80 |
  | Mac and cheese dinner | 100 |
  | Milkshake | 140 |
  | 1 hour of video games | 160 |
  | Movie night | 220 |
  | McDonald's with a Sprite | 260 |
  | Playtime with a favorite friend | 320 |
  | Play City | 500 |
  | Bullwinkle's | 700 |

- **Certificates**: getting a prize spends the points and makes a trophy certificate she shows her parents. Past certificates stay under **My trophies**.
- **Send to my parents**:
  - If no parent numbers are saved yet, the game asks for them right there (a grown-up can help). They're also editable in the Grown-ups corner and stay only on the device.
  - She picks **Mom**, **Dad**, or **Both**.
  - First she **learns the number**: it shows big on screen and is read aloud. When she taps **I know it. Hide it!**, she types it from memory on a number pad. With Both, she learns and types each number in turn.
  - Misses help her learn: after two, green and red digits show which numbers are right. After three, she can peek for 3 seconds.
  - Then the share sheet sends the certificate picture. If a phone can't share pictures, there's a **Message** button for each parent and a **Save the picture** button.

### Unlocking

- **Teams** unlock by winning: beat a team to open the next one. Locked teams say which team to beat.
- **Hangar items** (paint colors, droid heads) cost credits (★). Each locked item shows how many more ★ she needs.

## Learning

Problems follow the California Common Core standards for grade 3:

| Skill | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Multiplication & division | facts within 100, with a dot-array "Show me" hint |
| Add & subtract to 1,000 | 2- and 3-digit, with regrouping |
| Rounding | nearest 10 and nearest 100 |
| Fractions | name the shaded part of a bar |
| Area & perimeter | square-unit grids and labeled rectangles |
| Time | elapsed time to the nearest 5 minutes, with a clock |
| Two-step word problems | multiply, then add or subtract |

The game adapts: skills she misses show up more often, and missed problems come back later as practice. Wrong answers show the correct answer and a one-line explanation.

## Grown-ups corner

Press and hold the **Grown-ups** button on the title screen for 1.5 seconds to open it. It shows accuracy by skill and recently missed problems, and lets you:

- **Learning tracker**: percent right in each subject with a 2-week trend, the question types she's doing great at and the ones that need practice, types she hasn't tried yet, and a suggestion for her weakest or least-practiced subject. **Practice more (subject) in every game** makes about half of all questions that subject until you tap **Stop focusing**.
- choose whether games mix subjects (default) or ask only their own subject
- set the level (Easier, 3rd grade, Challenge)
- set how often math pops up (once per turn, once plus a few, or lots)
- turn skills on or off
- see spelling, vocabulary, and reading accuracy and recently missed questions
- paste this week's spelling list (used by Hover-Soccer in Chapter 2)
- set what wrong answers cost: nothing, 1 ★, 2 ★ (default), or 1 trophy point. It never goes below zero, and Rescue Pod retries are free
- manage prizes: daily trophy point limit, parent phone numbers (stored only on the device), prize names and costs, bonus points, and which prizes have been given
- the Prize Shop shows how many points she has already spent on prizes, since getting a prize uses its points
- reset one game (levels, story, and learning history for Baseball, Soccer, Basketball, Space Battle, or Rescue; keeps her Star Knight, ship, and credits)
- reset everything for the signed-in player (other players are not changed)
